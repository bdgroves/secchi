Wildfire in the water

Smoke deposition delivers nutrients to the lake; nutrients feed algae; algae erode clarity. Overlaying NASA FIRMS active-fire detections and HRRR-Smoke plume forecasts on the chlorophyll and turbidity signals above lets us watch a fire's fingerprint arrive in the lake.
