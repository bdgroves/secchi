**Subject:** Follow-up from an outside TEON data user: one station about to lose power, and some data issues

Hi TEON team,

I wrote earlier about dissolved-oxygen saturation. Since mid-September I've kept a public archive of the TEON API (brooksgroves.com/secchi; code at github.com/bdgroves/secchi), and a few things have turned up that seem worth passing on. The first is time-sensitive.

**1. Two stations aren't being charged: Glenbrook 1 and Glenbrook 5.**
A charging battery reaches about 14 V on a sunny day, and over the last two weeks of September every other station peaked at 14.0–14.4 V. Glenbrook 1 never passed 11.5 V and Glenbrook 5 never passed 12.4 V.

Glenbrook 5 has had no charging since June 2026. Its voltage collapsed in May and again in early June (the June outage, whose data never came back), recovered after what look like battery swaps in mid-May and early September, and is falling again, to an overnight low of 11.55 V. At the last cycle's rate it would run down around early December.

Glenbrook 1's daily charging swing disappeared in July 2025. Since then its supply voltage has only fallen, apart from a jump in mid-January 2026 that looks like a battery swap, and it was 11.40 V on September 28, falling about 0.05 V a week. It never reaches a charging voltage, even in midsummer, which suggests a failed solar panel or charge controller. For comparison, Glenbrook 4's daily peaks rose to about 14.4 V from September 2025 and its winter held above 12 V, which looks like exactly the repair Glenbrook 1 needs. If Glenbrook 1 runs flat the logger will stop, and at Glenbrook 4 the outages that followed battery collapses in winter 2024–25 never came back upstream.

**2. Two sondes with channels in the wrong columns.**
- **Sunnyside, 2026-04-30 11:30 to 06-25 08:00**, every channel: the temperature field reads 85–102 (it holds the saturation), the saturation field holds the temperature, and depth, salinity and turbidity are jumbled too — 5,145 records.
- **4H Camp, 2026-07-17 14:15 to 07-24 13:15**, the optical channels: Do_mgL holds the saturation (about 86), Do_percent the chlorophyll (about 0.65), Turbidity the concentration (about 7.7). One pH reading is 89.4.

Both look like service visits where sensors or ports changed while the parser kept the old order. April 30 is also the day Blackwood 3 shows a visit, and Sunnyside's scramble ends with a few odd readings on the morning of June 25. The values look intact, so they may be recoverable by remapping.

**3. Dissolved-oxygen saturation (update to my earlier note).**
All five EXO sondes, now including 4H Camp, report saturation referenced to sea level: each fits the sea-level calculation to 0.004 mg/L (162,201 readings), while the MiniDOTs correctly use lake pressure. At 1,898 m that makes EXO saturation read about 78–85 % where it is really about 99–107 %.

**4. Outages, and good news.**
Thank you: Blackwood 2's whole outage came back. It uploaded 9,519 readings from June 18 to September 25, every 15-minute reading in those 99 days. Glenbrook 5's three-day gap at the end of September filled completely, and Glenbrook 4's a week earlier. Two questions remain: Blackwood 2 hasn't reported since September 25 at noon, and Glenbrook 2 is back but its September 22–28 readings haven't arrived. Its rain gauge stopped separately, on August 14. Glenbrook 4's older outages, about 68 days in 2024–25, never came back; the winter ones followed battery collapses, but a 21-day one from October 15, 2025 happened on a healthy battery.

It was also good to see new tree-stress sensors at Blackwood 2 and Homewood.

**5. The watershed soil pH layer.**
22 of the 60 catchments average below pH 4.5 (Cave Rock 1.79). Their standard deviations suggest empty cells were averaged in as zero: a mix of zeros and real soil predicts both the mean and the spread, and they match. Cave Rock's 1.79 ± 2.75 is a 70/30 mix of 0 and pH 6.0. Worked through, 38 of 60 catchments contain zeros, and all come out at pH 5.6–6.4 once removed, in line with the clean ones. So it looks recoverable from the source raster.

**6. Smaller items**
- pH reads plausibly on Blackwood 3 and Meeks (about 7.8–7.9), but is 0 for much of the record at Glenbrook and 4H Camp and is no longer reported at Sunnyside.
- Turbidity zero offsets: Sunnyside about −2.1 and 4H Camp about −2.4.
- Service visits aren't flagged: Blackwood 3 has six and a half hours of handling readings on April 30, 2026.
- Glenbrook 5's air temperature and humidity probe was offline from early June to mid-August 2025 while its soil sensors kept logging.
- Marlette Creek's TWI of 833.9 looks out of range.

I'm happy to share the queries or data behind any of this. Thanks for building the network and opening it up; it has been a pleasure to work with.
