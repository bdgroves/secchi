**Subject:** Follow-up from an outside TEON data user: one station about to lose power, and a few data issues

Hi TEON team,

I wrote earlier about dissolved-oxygen saturation. Since mid-September I've kept a public archive of the TEON API (brooksgroves.com/secchi; code at github.com/bdgroves/secchi), and a few things have turned up that seem worth passing on. The first is time-sensitive.

**1. Glenbrook 1's battery isn't being charged.**
Its supply voltage has fallen every week since mid-January, from 12.74 V (just after what looks like a battery swap the week of January 12) to 11.40 V on September 28, with no daily charging rise, even in June and July. That looks like a failed solar panel or charge controller. It's falling about 0.05 V a week now. If it runs flat the logger will stop, and at Glenbrook 4 the outages that followed battery collapses in winter 2024–25 never came back upstream. A panel or controller check, or a battery swap, soon would probably save the record.

**2. 4H Camp: optical channels in the wrong columns, July 2026.**
Glad to see 4H Camp public now. For about six days ending July 24, 2026 (564 records), its optical values appear under each other's names: Do_mgL reads about 85.8 (the saturation value), Do_percent about 0.65 (the chlorophyll value), and Turbidity about 7.7 (the concentration). Temperature, conductivity and depth look normal, and one pH reading is 89.4. It looks like sensors were moved between ports while the parser kept the old order, so the data may be recoverable by remapping.

**3. Dissolved-oxygen saturation (update to my earlier note).**
All five EXO sondes, now including 4H Camp, report saturation referenced to sea level: each fits the sea-level calculation to 0.004 mg/L (162,201 readings), while the MiniDOTs correctly use lake pressure. At 1,898 m that makes EXO saturation read about 78–85 % where it is really about 99–107 %.

**4. Stations currently dark.**
Blackwood 2 (last reading June 18, 2026) and Glenbrook 2 (September 22). Both batteries were healthy at their last reading (12.7–12.9 V), so it looks like telemetry or hardware rather than power. Glenbrook 4 dropped out September 23–24 and recovered every reading when it reconnected. Do the loggers buffer during a dropout? Its earlier outages, about 68 days in 2024–25, never came back; the winter ones followed battery collapses to around 7–8 V, but a 21-day outage from October 15, 2025 happened on a healthy battery.

**5. Smaller items**
- pH reads plausibly on Blackwood 3 and Meeks (about 7.8–7.9), but is 0 for much of the record at Glenbrook and 4H Camp and is no longer reported at Sunnyside.
- Turbidity zero offsets: Sunnyside about −2.1 and 4H Camp about −2.4.
- Blackwood 3 has a few hours in April 2026 of warm, low-oxygen readings that look like a service visit. Flagging service windows would help anyone using the data.
- Glenbrook 5's air temperature and humidity probe was offline from early June to mid-August 2025 while its soil sensors kept logging.
- Watershed layer: soil pH is 1.79 at Cave Rock, which suggests no-data cells were averaged as zero; if so, other catchments may be biased low too. Marlette Creek's TWI of 833.9 also looks out of range.

I'm happy to share the queries or data behind any of this. Thanks for building the network and opening it up; it has been a pleasure to work with.
